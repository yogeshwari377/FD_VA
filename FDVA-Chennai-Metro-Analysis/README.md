# Chennai Metro Rail Limited (CMRL) – Transit & Urban Catchment Analysis

## Project Overview

This project presents a comprehensive data-driven analysis of the Chennai Metro Rail Limited (CMRL) transit network using Python and data visualization techniques.

The study explores passenger ridership trends, station-level performance, ticketing patterns, geographical distribution, and the relationship between urban catchment characteristics and metro usage.

## Objectives

- Analyze daily, hourly, and monthly passenger ridership.
- Identify peak travel hours and commuter patterns.
- Compare weekday and weekend passenger traffic.
- Analyze Blue Line and Green Line performance.
- Examine digital ticketing adoption and fare-mix transitions.
- Explore relationships between urban catchment features and ridership.
- Develop interactive dashboards and geospatial visualizations.

2. Data cleaning and preprocessing.

6. Urban catchment correlation analysis.
7. Interactive dashboard development.
8. Geospatial visualization.
9. Interpretation of findings and conclusions.

## Key Findings

The notebook highlights several insights:

- Weekday ridership is higher than weekend ridership.
- The Blue Line carries a higher average passenger volume than the Green Line.
- Morning and evening hours show concentrated commuter demand.
- Urban catchment characteristics are examined in relation to station ridership.
- Ticketing analysis provides insights into passenger adoption of different payment methods.
